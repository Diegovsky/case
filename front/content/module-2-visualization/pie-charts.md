# Pie Charts (Sector Graphs)

Pie charts represent "parts of a whole." They are essentially circles divided into slices.

## Reading Pie Charts
- **Area/Angle:** The size of the slice is proportional to the quantity it represents.
- **Total:** The entire circle always represents 100% or the total sum.

## Crucial Conversions
In ENEM, you often need to convert between percentages and degrees:
- $100\% = 360^\circ$
- $1\% = 3.6^\circ$
- $25\% = 90^\circ$

**Example:** If a slice represents 20% of the total, its central angle is $0.20 \cdot 360^\circ = 72^\circ$.

💡 **ENEM Tip:** If the chart is missing a value, subtract the known percentages from 100% to find the remainder.
