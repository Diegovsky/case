# Table Analysis

Tables are a structured way to present large amounts of data. Many ENEM questions are "treasure hunts" where you must find the right cell.

## Effective Reading Strategy
1. **Headers:** Read the top row and left column carefully to understand what the rows and columns represent.
2. **Units:** Check if the data is in thousands, millions, or percentages.
3. **Cross-Referencing:** Find the intersection of the required row and column.

## Common Tasks
- **Extracting data for calculations:** Finding two values in a table to calculate a mean or a ratio.
- **Comparing rows/columns:** Identifying which category has the highest or lowest value.

💡 **ENEM Tip:** Don't let the size of the table intimidate you. Most of the data is "noise"; look for the specific keys mentioned in the question.
