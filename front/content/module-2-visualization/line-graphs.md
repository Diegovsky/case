# Line Graphs

Line graphs are primarily used to show **trends over time** (time series).

## Key Features
- **Slope:** A steep line indicates a rapid change; a flat line indicates stability.
- **Peaks and Troughs:** Highest and lowest points in the timeline.
- **Trendlines:** General direction (upward, downward, or fluctuating).

## Analysis Strategy
When analyzing a line graph in the exam:
1. Identify the time interval.
2. Look for the point of maximum growth or decline.
3. Compare different lines if the graph shows multiple datasets.

💡 **ENEM Tip:** Pay attention to whether the graph shows absolute values or percentage change.
