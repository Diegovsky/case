# Median (Mediana)

The median is the middle value of a dataset when the data is ordered.

## How to find the Median
1. **Order the data:** Arrange the values in ascending or descending order.
2. **Find the middle:**
   - **Odd number of terms:** The median is the exact middle value.
   - **Even number of terms:** The median is the average of the two middle values.

## Examples
**Odd dataset:** $\{3, 5, 7, 12, 15\}$
The median is **7**.

**Even dataset:** $\{3, 5, 7, 12, 15, 20\}$
The middle values are 7 and 12.
$$	ext{Median} = \frac{7 + 12}{2} = 9.5$$

💡 **ENEM Tip:** The median is less affected by "outliers" (extremely high or low values) than the mean is.
