# Arithmetic Mean (Média Aritmética)

The arithmetic mean is the most common way to find the "average" of a set of data.

## Simple Arithmetic Mean
To calculate the simple mean, sum all the values in the dataset and divide by the total number of values.

**Formula:**
$$	ext{Mean} = \frac{\sum x_i}{n}$$

**Example:**
A student's grades in four tests are 7, 8, 5, and 10.
$$	ext{Mean} = \frac{7 + 8 + 5 + 10}{4} = \frac{30}{4} = 7.5$$

## Weighted Mean (Média Ponderada)
In many ENEM questions, some values are more important than others. This is where the weighted mean comes in. Each value is multiplied by a "weight" before being summed.

**Formula:**
$$	ext{Weighted Mean} = \frac{\sum (x_i \cdot w_i)}{\sum w_i}$$

**Example:**
A student has a grade of 6 in Math (weight 3) and 9 in English (weight 1).
$$	ext{Weighted Mean} = \frac{(6 \cdot 3) + (9 \cdot 1)}{3 + 1} = \frac{18 + 9}{4} = \frac{27}{4} = 6.75$$

💡 **ENEM Tip:** Always check if the question mentions "weights" or "percentages" associated with the values.
