# Simple Probability

Probability is the measure of how likely an event is to occur.

## The Fundamental Formula
$$	ext{Probability} = \frac{	ext{Favorable Outcomes}}{	ext{Total Possible Outcomes}}$$

## Example
If you roll a fair six-sided die, what is the probability of getting an even number?
- **Favorable Outcomes**: $\{2, 4, 6\}$ (3 outcomes)
- **Total Possible Outcomes**: $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$ (6 outcomes)
- $P(	ext{Even}) = \frac{3}{6} = 0.5$ or $50\%$

💡 **ENEM Tip:** Always define your sample space (the "Total") first. A common mistake is using the wrong total.
