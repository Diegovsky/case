# Range

The range (or amplitude) is the simplest measure of how spread out a dataset is.

## Definition
The range is the difference between the maximum value and the minimum value in a set.

**Formula:**
$$	\text{Range} = 	\text{Max} - 	\text{Min}$$

## Example
In a group of students, the ages are $\{15, 16, 17, 17, 18, 21\}$.
$$	\text{Range} = 21 - 15 = 6 	\text{ years.}$$

💡 **ENEM Tip:** While the range gives a quick idea of the spread, it is very sensitive to outliers.
