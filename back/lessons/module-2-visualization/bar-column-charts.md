# Bar and Column Charts

Bar charts are used to compare quantities across different categories.

## How to read them
- **X-Axis (Categories):** Usually shows the groups being compared.
- **Y-Axis (Values):** Shows the quantity or percentage.
- **Height/Length:** The height of the bar directly represents the value.

## Common ENEM Traps
1. **Broken Y-Axis:** Sometimes the Y-axis doesn't start at 0 to exaggerate differences. Always check the axis start!
2. **Scale:** Ensure you are reading the increments correctly (e.g., is each line 10 units or 100 units?).

💡 **ENEM Tip:** Bar charts are perfect for finding the **Mode** (the tallest bar).
